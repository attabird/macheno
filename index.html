controls

  • tap empty sky — plant a star
  • ctrl/cmd+tap empty sky — plant the selected drum
  • 7–9 or the kick/snare/hat buttons — choose which drum you plant next
  • drag a drum up/down — damps it: kick goes big open to tight, snare fat to crisp, hat open to closed; left/right still sets its speed
  • tap a star — start or hush its pattern
  • double-tap a star — remove it
  • drag a star — up/down retunes pitch, left/right changes speed; dragging it around also stretches its sustain
  • flick a star (or a selected group) — throw it; it bounces off the edges with little plucks until it settles
  • scroll on a star — add or remove grain
  • shift+drag a star — raise or lower its volume
  • drag empty sky — select stars and drums, then drag one to move the group
  • \ — delete the selection
  • m — mute or unmute the whole selection at once
  • space — pause the beat (sounds already ringing keep their sustain); press again to pick up where it left off
  • shift+drag empty sky — draw a drone note as long as the line, then it repeats (full width holds it constant)
  • drag a drone — up/down retunes it, left/right changes its pulse speed and how slowly it swells in
  • scroll on a drone — speed up or slow down its filter wobble
  • alt+scroll on a drone — shift its filter into a higher or lower register
  • shift+drag a drone — raise or lower its volume
  • double-tap a drone — remove it
  • 1–6 or the dots below — choose the next star's color
  • grid below — while on, new or dragged stars/drums snap to the sixteenth-note columns and lock to one 2-second bar; the brighter lines are the four downbeats
  • < key > below — change what key everything plays in; chromatic frees it from any scale
  • guide below — hide or show this panel
